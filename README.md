# GE Vernova | Manufacturing Quality & Systems Analysis

**Through the Eyes of a Mathematics Graduate**

## Project Overview

This report documents my observations during a placement with GE Vernova's Quality organisation in Rugby, UK.

Approaching an unfamiliar industrial environment from a mathematical perspective, I explored how complex organisations identify risk, allocate resources, investigate failures and transform information into sustainable improvements.

The objective was not to audit or evaluate GE Vernova's operations, but to understand how experienced professionals approach uncertainty, problem-solving and decision-making.

## Key Areas Explored

### 1. Risk Prioritisation & Resource Optimisation
- Allocation of limited inspection and Quality resources.
- Risk-based supplier monitoring and performance indicators.
- The relationship between operational exposure, resource constraints and decision-making.

### 2. Non-Conformance & Root-Cause Analysis
- Non-Conformance Reports (NCRs) and supplier investigations.
- Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA).
- Risk Priority Numbers (RPNs) and the 6M Cause Assessment Matrix.
- Translating identified failures into corrective and preventive actions.

### 3. Quality Management & Organisational Systems
- ISO 9001 and Quality Management Systems.
- Internal auditing, process ownership and competency management.
- Knowledge transfer, organisational learning and continuous improvement.

### 4. Manufacturing Inspection & Measurement Reliability
- Factory Quality Assurance and inspection processes.
- Critical-to-Quality characteristics and GO/NO-GO gauges.
- Measurement System Analysis (MSA), including accuracy, precision and repeatability.
- The importance of understanding measurement uncertainty before relying on quantitative indicators.

### 5. From Detection to Prediction

The report explores how organisations can progress from identifying problems to anticipating future risks:

**Detect → Measure → Understand → Prevent → Sustain → Predict**

## Mathematical Perspective

Throughout the placement, I connected industrial Quality practices with broader analytical concepts:

- **Optimisation:** Allocating constrained resources to areas of greatest risk.
- **Risk Modelling:** Translating qualitative assessments into structured measures.
- **Systems Thinking:** Understanding dependencies, feedback loops and information flow.
- **Measurement Uncertainty:** Evaluating the reliability of the data underlying decisions.
- **Decision-Making:** Distinguishing between a numerical metric and the assumptions behind it.
